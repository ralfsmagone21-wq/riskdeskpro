FAQ
Is my password safe?
Use your investor (read-only) password. It's sent over HTTPS to provision a MetaAPI connection and is never stored in the browser.
Can this place trades for me?
No. RiskDesk only reads balance/equity and calculates a lot size — it never sends trade orders.
Which brokers work?
Any MT4 or MT5 broker. Pick from 38 preloaded brokers or enter a custom server.
Can I cancel Pro or Elite?
Yes, subscriptions are managed through Stripe and can be cancelled anytime.
